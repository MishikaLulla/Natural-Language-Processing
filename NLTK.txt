Text preprocessing is the process of cleaning and preparing raw text before giving it to a machine learning or NLP model
4 steps are
tokenization
Stemming - reducing words to their base form by removing prefixes or suffixes
Lemmatization- reduces a word to its base form  but it uses language dictionaries to produce a meaningful word

*******Tokenization
process of breaking text into smaller units called tokens A token can be a word sentence or character

Types of Tokenization

Word Tokenization
Breaks a sentence into individual words

"I love learning Python."
["I", "love", "learning", "Python", "."]

Sentence Tokenization
Breaks a paragraph into individual sentences.

"I love Python NLP is interesting."
→["I love Python.", "NLP is interesting."]

Character Tokenization:
Breaks a word into individual characters


use-
Makes text easier to process
Helps analyze individual words or sentences
first steps in NLP preprocessing
Useful for tasks such as sentiment analysis, text classification, and translation

******Stop Word Removal
words that may not provide much useful information for certain NLP tasks

Why 
Reduces the amount of unnecessary text
Makes processing faster
Reduces the number of words the model needs to consider
Can help some NLP tasks focus on more meaningful words


*****Stemming
 process of reducing words to a root or stem by removing prefixes or suffixes
 breaking down the words into their base form, runs=run

why
 
Groups different forms of words together
Reduces the number of unique words
Makes text processing simpler and fast


Drawbacks of Stemming

May produce meaningless words
It can remove too many letters and create words that aren't actual words
Example: studies → studi
Loss of meaning
Different words may be reduced to the same stem even when their meanings are different
Over-stemming
Sometimes too much of the word is removed
Under-stemming
Sometimes related words are not reduced to the same stem
Doesn't understand context
Stemming mainly uses rules to cut word endings It doesn't understand the actual meaning or grammar of a sentence


to overcome this we use lemmatization

*****Lemmatization

process of converting a word into its meaningful dictionary base form called a lemma
meaning of word is retained 

why

Produces meaningful base words
Groups different forms of the same word
Takes the context and grammatical information into account
Useful when the actual meaning of words is important





**** difference 
| Stemming                                             Lemmatization                       
| 
| Removes prefixes/suffixes using simple rules  Finds the meaningful dictionary base form  |
| Does not understand the meaning of the word  | Uses linguistic knowledge and context     |
| Faster                                       | Comparatively slower                      |
| Less accurate                                | More accurate                             |
| May produce meaningless words                | Produces valid words                      |
| `studies → studi`                            | `studies → study`                         |
| `running → run`                              | `running → run`                           |
